# Matsya Avtaar

### 30-Minute AI-Assisted Mythological Episode

**Matsya Avtaar** is a long-form mythological episode developed as an exploration of AI-assisted entertainment production.

The project demonstrates the application of Generative AI across a longer narrative format, combining storytelling, visual development, scene creation and post-production into a complete episode.

##  Project Concept

The episode explores the story of **Matsya Avtaar**, the first incarnation of Lord Vishnu, through a long-form visual storytelling format.

The creative objective was to explore how Generative AI can support the production of mythology-driven entertainment while maintaining a consistent visual world across an extended narrative.

---

##  Creative Development

The production involved:

- Story development
- Narrative structuring
- Character development
- Visual development
- Scene planning
- Shot design
- AI-assisted visual generation
- Video generation
- Voice and audio integration
- Editing
- Final episode production
