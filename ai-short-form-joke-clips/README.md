# AI Short-Form Joke Clips

### AI-Assisted Short-Form Entertainment Experiments

A collection of short-form comedic clips created as experiments in AI-assisted entertainment production.

The project explores how short comedy concepts can be developed from an idea or joke into an animated visual piece using Generative AI.

---

##  Watch the Clip

 **[Watch the AI Joke Clips on YouTube](https://youtu.be/F0-5LkADYLU)**

---

##  Format

The clips are designed around:

- Short comedic situations
- Strong visual gags
- Character-based humour
- Quick narrative setups
- Visual punchlines
- Short-form pacing

##  Project Overview

These experiments focus on creating short, humorous visual stories with a stylized animated aesthetic.

Each clip explores the combination of:

- Comedy writing
- Visual storytelling
- Character development
- AI-generated visuals
- Animation
- AI voice
- Music and sound
- Editing

---

##  Creative Approach

The workflow starts with a simple comedic idea and develops it into a short visual narrative.

```text
Joke / Idea
    ↓
Script
    ↓
Visual Concept
    ↓
Character Development
    ↓
Scene Design
    ↓
AI Image Generation
    ↓
AI Animation
    ↓
Voice
    ↓
Music & Sound Effects
    ↓
Final Edit
